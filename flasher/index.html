🔧 Web Flasher

Flash your board over USB, straight from the browser — no toolchain. Chrome/Edge on desktop.

This browser can't flash over USB — use Chrome or Edge on desktop. Open this page over https.

Before you click Install

  • Connect with a USB-C data cable and pick the serial port in the popup.
  • If the board runs USB-HID (no serial), or has no BOOT button, put it in download mode first (hold BOOT + tap RESET, or tie GPIO0→GND while plugging).
  • Updating? Leave Erase device unticked — settings/WiFi live in a separate partition and are preserved.
  • On an ESP32-C5, unplug/replug after flashing (it doesn't auto-reboot).
